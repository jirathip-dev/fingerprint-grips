How do I measure?
  1. Palm up, hand flat, fingers straight. A ruler or calipers, in millimetres.
  2. Length: the crease where the finger meets the palm, to the very tip.
  3. Middle: middle knuckle crease to the end-joint crease.
  4. Tip: end-joint crease to the very tip.
  5. Width: widest point of the tip segment, side to side.
  6. Measure twice and use the average. Half millimetres are fine.
