How do I measure?
  1. Palm up, hand flat, fingers straight. A ruler or calipers, in millimetres.
  2. Length: the crease where the finger meets the palm, to the very tip.
  3. Middle: middle-joint crease to the end-joint crease. If a crease has two lines, measure from the middle of the two.
  4. Tip: end-joint crease to the very tip.
  5. Width: widest point of the tip section, side to side.
  6. Measure twice and use the average. You can enter half millimetres (like 18.5).
